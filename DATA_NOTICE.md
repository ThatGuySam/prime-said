# Data and media notice

The MIT license applies to this project's code and original documentation. It does not relicense third-party material.

## Repository data

Prime Said may contain factual metadata, machine-generated transcripts, timestamp alignments, short quotations needed for search and verification, and human corrections. Contributors must identify the source and may not represent transcript data as an official publication by the speaker.

Transcripts are fallible. The video at the linked timestamp is the source of truth. Search cards must label uncertain words when confidence is low and must not silently turn an uncertain transcript into a definitive quotation.

## Video and audio

Downloaded media is temporary ingestion input. It must not be committed or redistributed. Production pages embed or link to media hosted by its original platform. Thumbnails should use platform-hosted URLs where permitted or generated, rights-safe visual treatments.

## Names and marks

ThePrimeagen, YouTube, Twitch, Cloudflare, and other names or marks belong to their respective owners. Their use is descriptive. Prime Said is an unofficial fan project and does not imply endorsement.

## Removal and correction requests

Open a repository issue with the affected Prime Said URL, source URL, and requested action. Maintainers may remove a source, quote, transcript segment, or page without preserving deleted text. A creator or rights holder request receives priority. See `docs/seo-attribution-takedown.md` for the operating policy.
